# Design Inspiration Index — Full Site Catalog

> Companion to `SKILL.md`. 55+ sites grouped by category. For each: URL, what it is, and **what to extract**.
> Verified Aug 2026. Where a site has shifted, the current state is noted.

---

## A. Award galleries (judged / trending — highest signal-to-noise)

These are *judged* or *editor-curated* — a panel or curator already filtered the noise. Start here for visual language and "what good looks like right now."

### 1. Awwwards — https://awwwards.com
- **What:** The largest judged web-design award panel. Site of the Day / Month / Year, plus developer, honor, and mobile awards. Judged on Design, Usability, Creativity, Content.
- **Extract:** Overall composition, typography pairings, hero treatments, navigation patterns, scroll/choreography, color systems, and the *technologies used* (filterable: GSAP, WebGL, Three.js, Lenis, Framer, Next.js, Webflow, Svelte). Use the Tags + Categories + Colors + Technologies filters to narrow to your page kind. Best source for "premium/agency/experimental" direction.
- **Caveat:** Awwwards sites optimize for spectacle over conversion. Borrow composition + motion vocabulary, not the conversion-blind layouts.

### 2. CSS Design Awards — https://cssdesignawards.com
- **What:** Judged gallery with Website of the Day and a Web Awards Gallery. Judge scores broken out as **UI / UX / Innovation** — uniquely useful because you can see *why* a site scored well.
- **Extract:** The UI/UX/Innovation breakdown itself is a rubric — use it as a self-check. Filter the gallery by style, subject, color. Good for cross-checking Awwwards picks (second opinion on taste).

### 3. The FWA — https://thefwa.com
- **What:** Pioneering interactive/innovation awards. Lean toward experimental, WebGL, game-like, and brand experiences.
- **Extract:** Cutting-edge interaction concepts and the limits of what is currently technically possible. Skip for B2B/trust-first work; gold for "wow" brand moments.

### 4. CSS Light — https://csslight.com
- **What:** Judged gallery, lighter curation volume.
- **Extract:** Cross-reference picks; good for finding sites that slipped past Awwwards.

### 5. Webby Awards — https://webbyawards.com
- **What:** Broad internet awards across sites, apps, social, video, advertising. Categories span more than pure web design.
- **Extract:** Categorical winners (Best Navigation, Best Visual Design, Best UX) — these named-category winners are tighter references than the overall winners.

### 6. BestWebsite.gallery — https://bestwebsite.gallery
- **What:** Curated by David Hellmann. Filterable by color, CMS, style, framework.
- **Status:** SOTD picks stalled after Nov 2024; archive + filters still usable.
- **Extract:** The color + framework filters are the value. Use it as a search tool ("show me Tailwind + dark + minimal") rather than a freshness feed.

## B. Landing-page / marketing-site galleries

For hero composition, section sequencing, pricing layout, social proof patterns, and CTA treatment.

### 7. Land-book — https://land-book.com
- **What:** Landing-page gallery with thematic **Collections** (curated groupings around a theme).
- **Extract:** Collections are pre-baked moodboards — pull a whole collection matching your brief. Good for section sequencing and hero/CTA patterns.

### 8. Landingfolio — https://landingfolio.com
- **What:** Landing-page inspiration **plus 800+ copy-paste components** for Tailwind / Webflow / Figma, organized by industry (SaaS, Product, Business, Marketing, Design Tools…).
- **Extract:** Both the page-level layout *and* the actual component code/figma. Categorization by industry means you can match a SaaS vs. consumer vs. dev-tool tone quickly.

### 9. One Page Love — https://onepagelove.com
- **What:** Since 2008; 9,000+ single-page sites and 8,500+ page sections. Filters: genre (portfolio, landing, app), style (minimal, illustrative, brutalism), platform (Webflow, Framer, WordPress), tech (Tailwind, GSAP, Next.js), color.
- **Extract:** The **page sections** library is gold — individual hero/feature/pricing/footer sections you can study in isolation. The tech filter lets you match your stack.

### 10. Lapa Ninja — https://lapa.ninja
- **What:** Landing-page captures + a large library of free design resources (mockups, illustrations, icons, UI kits).
- **Extract:** Full-page captures (see the whole flow in one image) and the free asset library.

### 11. Godly (now recent.design) — https://godly.website → https://recent.design
- **What:** Godly redirects (301) to **recent.design** — a high-curation feed (a few high-quality picks per cycle). Godly was known for tight 3–5/week curation of premium product/marketing sites.
- **Extract:** Recent.design continues the "what is shipping right now" signal. Best for current SaaS/dev-tool aesthetic baseline.

### 12. Curated.design — https://curated.design
- **What:** By Craftwork. Categorized catalog: Agency, Portfolio, AI, Tech, Web3, Design Tools, Dev Tools, Marketing, Productivity, E-commerce, Finance, Web/Desktop/Mobile Apps. Daily updates; Pro tier for bookmarks/ad-free.
- **Extract:** Category-first browsing — go straight to your app category (e.g. "Design Tools") and survey the competitive set.

### 13. SiteInspire — https://siteinspire.com
- **What:** Curating since 2010. Hand-picked, tagged by **style** (minimalist, flat, responsive), **type** (blog, portfolio, e-commerce), **subject** (fashion, architecture, food). European/editorial sensibility.
- **Extract:** The most consistently tasteful gallery for editorial, typographic, and restrained design. Filter style × subject to match a brief.

### 14. Httpster — https://httpster.net
- **What:** Opinionated curation biased to **independent designers, small studios, type foundries, and creative professionals**. Understated elegance.
- **Extract:** Portfolio and studio-site patterns; type-forward restraint. Partner-promotes Mobbin.

### 15. Minimal Gallery — https://minimal.gallery
- **What:** Focused exclusively on minimalist web design.
- **Extract:** When the brief is "calm/minimal/editorial/Linear-style," go here first to calibrate restraint — what does *true* minimal look like, so you don't underdo it.

### 16. Refero — https://refero.design
- **What:** SaaS / web product screenshots organized by page type and component.
- **Extract:** Real SaaS page patterns (pricing, settings, empty states, dashboards) — closer to shipped product than concept art.

### 17. Saas Landing — https://saaslanding.com (and similar SaaS-only galleries)
- **What:** SaaS-only landing pages.
- **Extract:** SaaS-specific conversion patterns — pricing tables, feature matrices, integration logos, testimonial layouts.

## C. App-screenshot / real-product-UI galleries (shipped product, not concept)

Use these when building product UI (dashboards, settings, lists, mobile screens). These are *real shipped apps*, not Dribbble shots — far higher fidelity to constraints.

### 18. Mobbin — https://mobbin.com
- **What:** The largest library of real iOS / Android / Web app screenshots, filterable by app, screen type, pattern, color. Includes flows.
- **Extract:** How market-leading apps actually handle navigation, onboarding, lists, settings, empty states, and dark mode. The single best source for mobile product UI. Filter by pattern ("onboarding", "paywall", "settings") and by app.

### 19. Page Flows — https://pageflows.com
- **What:** Screen recordings of complete user journeys (onboarding, checkout, login, booking, purchasing) with step-by-step annotations, plus browsable Screens, UI Elements, and an Email library. iOS / Android / Web.
- **Extract:** End-to-end flow logic — *why* screens sequence the way they do, where friction is removed, how progress is communicated. The UI Elements + Email sub-libraries are bonus component references. Featured apps: Revolut, Robinhood, Monzo, Spotify, Stripe, Notion, Slack, Duolingo.

### 20. Screenlane — https://screenlane.com
- **What:** Web + mobile UI patterns by category, with real-product screenshots.
- **Extract:** Pattern-level browsing ("show me 40 settings screens"). Good complement to Mobbin for web-heavy patterns.

### 21. UI Sources — https://uisources.com
- **What:** Real app flows and screens.
- **Extract:** Flow recordings and screens for interaction sequencing.

### 22. Pttrns — https://pttrns.com
- **What:** Mobile app screenshots organized by pattern (login, onboarding, profile, feed, etc.).
- **Extract:** Pattern-first mobile reference. Useful for "how do 30 apps do a profile screen."

### 23. Mobile Pattern — https://mobile-pattern.com
- **What:** Mobile UI patterns gallery.
- **Extract:** Pattern variations across real apps.

### 24. Inspired UI — https://inspiredui.com
- **What:** Mobile + web UI patterns.
- **Extract:** Cross-platform pattern comparison.

### 25. Collectui — https://collectui.com
- **What:** Daily UI organized across ~100 categories.
- **Extract:** Category drilling ("empty states", "404", "calendar pickers"). Note: more concept/Dribbble-adjacent than real product — treat as idea generation, not shipping reference.

### 26. Appshots / Scrns — https://appshots.co (and https://scrns.com)
- **What:** Curated app screenshot collections.
- **Extract:** Quick visual survey of a vertical.

## D. Component / pattern libraries (real, coded — copy with attribution)

These give you working code, not just screenshots. Use to match a real design system rather than reinventing.

### 27. Tailwind UI — https://tailwindui.com
- **What:** Official Tailwind component templates (React, Vue, Alpine), marketing + application UI.
- **Extract:** The canonical "Tailwind-native" component layout, spacing, and responsive patterns. Baseline for anything built on Tailwind.

### 28. shadcn/ui — https://ui.shadcn.com
- **What:** Radix primitives + Tailwind, copy-paste components you own. The de facto modern React primitive set.
- **Extract:** Accessible, composable primitives (Dialog, Combobox, DataTable, Command) — the assembly blocks for serious product UI.

### 29. Mantine — https://mantine.dev
- **What:** Full React component + hooks library with a polished default theme.
- **Extract:** Hooks (use-debounced-value, use-hotkeys, use-pagination) and complex components (RichTextEditor, DateInput, Combobox).

### 30. Park UI — https://park-ui.com
- **What:** Ark UI + Tailwind, headless components across React/Solid/Vue.
- **Extract:** Cross-framework headless patterns.

### 31. Aceternity — https://ui.aceternity.com
- **What:** Animated, "Awwwards-adjacent" React components (spotlights, glows, sticky scroll, 3D cards).
- **Extract:** High-impact marketing/hero motion components. **Caution:** these are very recognizable and now overused — the "AI-purple glow + spotlight card" cliché. Use sparingly and customize heavily.

### 32. 21st.dev — https://21st.dev
- **What:** Component registry, community-contributed.
- **Extract:** Browse for niche components; quality varies.

### 33. Magic UI — https://magicui.design
- **What:** Animated React components (marquees, bento grids, number tickers, shimmer buttons).
- **Extract:** Marketing motion components. Same overuse caution as Aceternity.

### 34. Geist (Vercel) — https://geist.vercel.com
- **What:** Vercel's design system + Geist font family.
- **Extract:** The "Linear/Vercel modern dev-tool" baseline — spacing scale, type, monochrome palette, button states.

## E. Motion / animation galleries + decoded patterns

For microinteraction timing, easing, and choreography — with code where possible.

### 35. UIMovement — https://uimovement.com (now https://medium.com/uimovement)
- **What:** Curated gallery of UI animation mockups, sortable by tags (Button, Chat, Menu, Payments, pull-to-refresh…). Weekly top-5 newsletter.
- **Extract:** Motion *ideas* and the physical-feel vocabulary (resistance, snap, overshoot). Treat as concept shots — verify feasibility before adopting.

### 36. UI Motion — https://uimotion.fyi
- **What:** 30+ decoded UI animation components across 10 categories, with code in CSS, React, and Framer Motion. Examples: Radial FAB fan-out, Magnetic Button, Perplexity-style Answer Stream, Duolingo Confetti, iOS Bottom Sheet, Twitter Swipe Tabs, iOS Pull-to-Refresh, Stripe Number Counter. New component weekly.
- **Extract:** **The single best source for animation *with code*.** Copy the timing/easing curves directly. E.g. Stripe counter: spring with `stiffness ~120, damping ~20`.

### 37. Micro Inspire — https://www.microinspire.me
- **What:** Ready-made microinteractions for Webflow, filterable by trigger/element/technology.
- **Extract:** Webflow-native interaction recipes and trigger mapping.

### 38. Detail (René Wang) — via uiuxshowcase.com
- **What:** Micro-moment studies with commentary on *why* a detail works (timing, resistance, subtle system behavior).
- **Extract:** The reasoning behind micro-decisions — taste and sensitivity, not just the technique.

### 39. Codrops — https://tympanus.net/codrops
- **What:** Experimental UI demos + full tutorials (SVG filters, WebGL, scroll effects, text effects).
- **Extract:** The *how-to* for advanced effects you saw on Awwwards. Searchable archive. Always check the date — some demos target older frameworks.

### 40. Lottiefiles — https://lottiefiles.com
- **What:** Lottie animation library + editor.
- **Extract:** Ready-made empty-state, loading, success, and onboarding animations. Tune speed/loop in the editor before dropping in.

### 41. Framer Motion examples — https://framermotion examples / docs
- **What:** Official Framer Motion example gallery + sandbox.
- **Extract:** Copy-paste animation recipes (layout animations, drag, gestures, AnimatePresence exit transitions) with the exact props.

## F. Community / portfolio galleries

For case-study depth and portfolio patterns.

### 42. Dribbble — https://dribbble.com
- **What:** Largest design community; shots with color search, tags, teams.
- **Extract:** Color palettes, shot-level polish, and *trend spotting*. **Caveat:** Dribbble shots are polished concepts, not shipped constraints — they routinely break accessibility, density, and real-data rules. Never copy a Dribbble shot into product UI without validating against real content and WCAG.

### 43. Behance — https://behance.net
- **What:** Full multi-image project case studies.
- **Extract:** Process documentation and case-study storytelling — how a designer frames the problem, exploration, and outcome. Useful for your own portfolio and for understanding design *rationale*.

### 44. Cofolios — https://www.cofolios.com
- **What:** Design *intern* portfolios from top tech companies (Apple, Meta, Google, Airbnb, Figma, Spotify…), plus AI case-study critique.
- **Extract:** The hiring bar for entry-level product design portfolios — storytelling structure, case-study sections, and visual standard. Use the AI critique to stress-test your own case-study images.

### 45. Muzli — https://muzli.com (Chrome extension + web)
- **What:** Design-news aggregator; surfaces Dribbble, Behance, Awwwards, and curated picks in one feed.
- **Extract:** Passive ambient trend awareness — install the extension for a "new tab = inspiration" habit.

## G. Design-system documentation (canonical references)

When the brief maps to a real, documented design system, reference the canonical tokens — don't reinvent.

### 46. Material 3 — https://m3.material.io
- **Extract:** Dynamic color, elevation tonal system, type scale, motion tokens.

### 47. Apple HIG — https://developer.apple.com/design
- **Extract:** iOS spacing, system materials, SF Symbols, motion characteristics, navigation patterns.

### 48. Polaris (Shopify) — https://polaris.shopify.com
- **Extract:** Commerce/merchant-facing patterns, dense data tables, form patterns, the Polaris token system.

### 49. Carbon (IBM) — https://carbondesignsystem.com
- **Extract:** Enterprise data-dense UI, grid, spacing scale, data-viz tokens.

### 50. Atlassian Design System — https://atlassian.design
- **Extract:** Collaboration-product patterns (comments, mentions, inline editing), tokens.

### 51. GDS / GOV.UK Design System — https://design-system.service.gov.uk
- **Extract:** The gold standard for accessible, trust-first, content-first design. Use for any public-sector / regulated / accessibility-critical brief.

### 52. USWDS (US Web Design System) — https://designsystem.digital.gov
- **Extract:** US federal accessibility-first patterns and tokens.

## H. Typography & color references (the supporting cast)

### 53. Typewolf — https://typewolf.com
- **Extract:** Trending type pairings and "what font is that" links. Use to escape the Inter-only default.

### 54. Fonts In Use — https://fontsinuse.com
- **Extract:** Real-world typography in shipped contexts (posters, packaging, sites) with pairing rationale.

### 55. Coolors — https://coolors.co
- **Extract:** Rapid palette generation + exploration; lock colors and vary others.

### 56. Realtime Colors — https://realtimecolors.com
- **Extract:** See a palette applied to a real layout in real time — catches the "looks great in swatches, dies on the page" problem.

### 57. Happy Hues — https://happyhue.co
- **Extract:** Palettes shown applied to actual components (hero, cards, buttons) — palette-in-context.

### 58. Color Hunt — https://colorhunt.co
- **Extract:** Community palettes; good for quick mood but validate contrast yourself.
